Before Landfall (Preparation)
  • Charge mobile phones, power banks, and emergency flashlights.
  • Store 3-4 days of clean drinking water in sealed jerry cans.
  • Keep dry food, baby milk, and essential prescription medications ready.
  • Fasten or move loose roofing sheets, zinc sheets, and potted plants indoors.
During Landfall (Active Storm)
  • Stay inside a pucca building or cyclone shelter away from glass windows.
  • CAUTION: Do NOT venture outside when the wind dies down suddenly; this is the storm eye and fierce reverse winds will resume quickly.
  • Turn off main electrical breaker and LPG gas cylinder valves.
After Landfall (Post-Storm Safety)
  • Stay clear of sagging electrical poles, snapped cables, and fallen trees.
  • Do not drive or wade across flooded tidal streams or culverts.
  • Wait for official ‘All Clear’ announcement from district authorities before returning home.

VAYU-DRISHTI User Guide

1

🌊 Public Citizen Safety View (No Login Needed)

Designed for coastal residents and the public. Directly view live storm severity, landfall countdowns, localized district danger levels, emergency do's and don'ts, and locate nearby verified cyclone shelters without creating an account.

2

🚨 Authority Incident Command Login

Restricted to official Disaster Management Authorities (NDMA, SDMA, District Administration). Authenticate to access restricted operations: Deep Multi-Horizon Track Forecasting, GIS Critical Infrastructure overlays, Landfall Shift Simulation, and Evacuation SITREP reports. (Use pre-filled chips for instant testing).

3

Live & Historical Observation

Use the toggle in the top-right header to switch between near-real-time satellite observation and verified historical storm replay (Amphan, Fani, Biparjoy, Tauktae, Yaas).

4

Monitor & Track Playback

Navigate to Monitor and choose a cyclone. Use the Previous / Play / Next controls or the timeline slider to track the cyclone eye, wind radii, and predicted corridor.

5

Scenario Simulation (What-If)

On the Simulation page, shift the projected track left or right and adjust the risk corridor width. The system recalculates impacted districts and population in real time.

6

Study & Side-by-Side Comparison

Visit Study for multi-sensor atmospheric metrics (SST, shear, pressure). Use Compare to benchmark two storms across duration, peak wind, and landfall impact.
